The work

A

The idea

Every six minutes, a tide gauge in the Tagus estuary takes a reading. Tidewrack turns each one into an instruction for the room: the height of the water sets the pitch, the speed of its rise and fall sets the density, and the slack water at every turn of the tide becomes silence.

  • Height → pitch Higher water, higher notes.

  • Speed → density When the tide runs fast, notes gather into chords.

  • Slack water → silence As the tide turns, the room falls quiet.

B

The space

Forty speakers hang at different heights in a converted harbour warehouse. There is no stage and no front row: you walk through the sound as it gathers, thins and moves overhead, the way the tide moves through the estuary outside.

C

The artist

Inês Varela is a composer born in Setúbal, on the Sado estuary, who works with environmental data. She treats each measurement as a note not yet played, and in Tidewrack she leaves the river to finish every phrase.
