# Ledgerline · Reconciliation overview

**Thesis.** A morning triage tool set like a ledger: health first, exception queue second, context third. Neutral paper, ink type and hairlines; colour is reserved for status and data.

- **Hierarchy.** A four-KPI strip; each delta has an arrow plus the word *better* or *worse*. The queue takes two-thirds of the width, with 12 rows in the first 1440×900 viewport. Trend and activity sit in a rail. Opening an exception swaps the rail for a sticky panel (ledger lines with a double-ruled difference, assign, status, resolve), so the queue stays visible.
- **Urgency is defined.** Open for 3+ days, or €5,000 and above, gets a flag shape and text. The "9 urgent" KPI filters the queue.
- **Numbers.** IBM Plex Sans tabular figures, right-aligned, two decimals; Plex Mono only for references. GBP/SEK amounts show, sort and total by their EUR equivalent.
- **Status never by colour alone.** Distinct icon shapes plus labels.
- **Responsive.** The table compacts by container width; below 1280 px the panel becomes a modal drawer; phones get a read-only urgent list.
- **Coherent data.** KPIs, deltas and chart derive from one 60-day series pinned to the brief's figures.
