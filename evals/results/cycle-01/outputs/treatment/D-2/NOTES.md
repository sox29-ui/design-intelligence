# Issue 14: Shade — design notes

**Thesis.** A reading-first issue home whose identity comes from the theme itself: shade is drawn, not decorated.

- **Direction.** Chosen over a giant-type cover or a plain contents page: a section drawing (Fig. 1: street ratio H:W = 2:1, sun at 63°, hatched shade) is both cover image and evidence for the essay. Hatching is the issue's only ornament.
- **Type.** Newsreader (optical sizes) for display and text; Archivo for labels. Body 20 px at every width, 1.6 leading, 27em measure: 59–64 characters per line on desktop, 40–45 on phones. Display 40–64 px, capped at 1440.
- **Layout.** Desktop reads like a drawing sheet: text on the left; figure, notes and pull quote in the margin. On phones the figure moves into the paragraph that discusses it and is reframed onto the street; navigation becomes an in-flow menu.
- **Colour.** Limewash paper, blue-black ink and "shadow blue" (a shaded white wall lit only by sky). Each back issue owns one colour.
- **Print.** Folios and the subscription offer tie the screen to the paper edition.
- **Access, weight.** Landmarks, skip link, visible focus, AA contrast, dark scheme, feedback-only motion; ~380 KB, no images.
