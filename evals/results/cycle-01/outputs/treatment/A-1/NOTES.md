# Tidewrack: design notes

**The page is a score's title page.** Title, composer and tempo mark (♩ = 6 min) sit above a full-bleed stave where one tide is written as notes. Water height sets pitch, its speed adds notes and slack water becomes rests. The same mapping drives an optional one-minute Web Audio sketch that only starts on request.

**Conversion first, on phones.** "Reserve a free timed entry" sits in the sticky header at every width and directly under the title, inside the first 390 px viewport. Each low-tide session links to a short form that preselects that evening.

**Data, not decoration.** Low-tide sessions are drawn as 19:30–21:30 timelines, so the silence lands at a different minute each night. Every graphic has a text equivalent.

**Type and colour.** Spectral is the composition's voice; Public Sans is the gauge's (labels, times, tabular figures). Fog paper and ink with one accent, wrack-line ochre, kept for low water and playback. The dark scheme is "the dim room".

**Restraint and weight.** Hairlines and space instead of cards, gradients, glass or scroll reveals. One 2.6 s entrance animation, off under reduced motion. About 166 KB total, CSS inlined, no images, AA contrast throughout.
