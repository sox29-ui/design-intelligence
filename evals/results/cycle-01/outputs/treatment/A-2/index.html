Tidewrack

A composition written by the tide, performed by the room.

The work

  1. I Written by the tide

    Every six minutes, a tide gauge in the Tagus estuary records the height of the water. Each reading becomes part of the score: the height sets the pitch, the speed of the rising or falling water sets the density, and slack water — the still moment when the tide turns — becomes silence. The composition is never finished. The tide keeps writing it.

  2. II Performed by the room

    Forty speakers hang at different heights through a converted warehouse on the river. There is no stage and no single place to listen from: you walk through the sound, and the piece shifts around you as you move. Seating is there if you would rather stay still.

  3. III Inês Varela

    Inês Varela is a composer born in Setúbal, on the Sado estuary. She works with environmental data, treating measurements as something to be listened to rather than read. For Tidewrack she wrote the rules; for six weeks, the water writes the notes.

Low-tide sessions
