# Ledgerline: Reconciliation overview

**Thesis.** A morning triage desk: read the totals, then work the queue. The identity comes from the product name: ledger rules, right-aligned tabular figures and a double rule under totals.

**Hierarchy.**
1. KPI totals band, with each delta written as "better" or "worse" as well as coloured.
2. Exception queue, sorted by priority (urgent means open 5+ days or €2,500 or more). It sorts by age or amount and filters by status. Arrow keys move between rows, Enter opens one, Esc closes it.
3. Trend chart and team journal. Selecting a row replaces them with the details: reconciliation legs, assign and resolve.

**Data.** Seeded sample data. Filters recompute every KPI, count and chart, and actions update them live.

**Chart.** Small multiples, because unmatched is about 3% of volume and would vanish if stacked. Direct labels, an incident note, a day readout and a data table.

**Colour.** Paper and ink, plus one blue for interactive elements. Status, deltas and unmatched volume use colour, always with an icon and text.

**Type.** IBM Plex Sans, with Plex Mono for IDs.

**Responsive.** At 768: menu sheet and modal details. At 390: KPIs, the urgent queue and activity.
