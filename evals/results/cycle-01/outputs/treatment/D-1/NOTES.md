# The Lighthouse Review — Issue 14: Shade

**Thesis.** An issue home that reads like the printed quarterly: authored, calm, built for long reading on phones. First read: the issue word *Shade* and the cover drawing; second: the cover essay; third: contents, interview, archive, subscriptions.

**Direction.** “Shade study”, chosen over an abstract cast-shadow cover (dark-hero and text-over-pattern risks) and a type-only contents page (generic). The cover is an architect’s street section at 13:00 (sun 76°, H : W ≈ 1.6) that carries the essay’s argument: inline SVG, recoloured at night, cropped to the street on phones.

**Type.** Literata (book face, optical sizes) for text and display; Schibsted Grotesk for labels and interface. 18 px body at every width, 1.6 leading, 36 rem measure (≈ 60 characters). Indented paragraphs, drop cap, margin notes and pull quote that fold inline on mobile. The italic *Shade* is the one oversized moment; everything else stays ≤ 3.3× body.

**Colour.** Limewash paper and ink; shade-blue accent; ochre only for fills and rules, never text. Night palette via `prefers-color-scheme`.

**Structure.** Hairlines, not cards; a numbered contents index; landmarks, skip link, one `h1`, 3 px focus rings; 120 ms state transitions only, behind reduced-motion. 293 KB total.
