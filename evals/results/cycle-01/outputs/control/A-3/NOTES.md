# Tidewrack: design notes

- **The page is a score.** The title is written on a five-line stave. Sections carry rehearsal marks (A–F). *Senza misura*, fermatas, *Da capo* and a final barline (*Fine*) continue the notation. Not tech, not festival: one serif (Newsreader, three static cuts, ~70 KB, non-blocking), mono labels for data, a dark estuary ground and one sodium-amber accent for "now", low water and the visitor.
- **Live, but honest.** A small harmonic tide model drives the hero reading, a twelve-hour score (height → pitch, speed → density, slack water → silence) and a one-minute Web Audio sketch. All labelled "modelled"; nothing autoplays; no media files.
- **Getting people to reserve.** "Reserve a free timed entry" sits in the hero, the header, a mobile dock (shown only between hero and form) and each low-tide session, which pre-selects its 20:30 slot. The booking form uses native radios, with a calendar showing closed Mondays and session nights, inline errors, and a ticket with calendar export. Slot times and the four-person limit are assumptions.
- **Access.** Skip link, landmarks, labelled fields, AA contrast, visible focus. Motion is off under `prefers-reduced-motion` and can be paused.
- **Weight.** About 28 KB gzipped HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks.
