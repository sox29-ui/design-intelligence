# The Lighthouse Review — Issue 14: Shade

**Concept.** The issue is treated as an object. Its cover is built from type: “Shade” stands on a paved horizon and casts its own shadow. A little JavaScript points the sun at the reader’s local time, and a slider moves it. Without JavaScript the cover stays at 16:00.

**Type.** Newsreader (a variable serif with optical sizes, made for screen reading) sets text and display; Instrument Sans handles wayfinding. The essay runs at 18–21px with about 1.6 leading: roughly 42 characters per line on phones, 60 on desktop. It has a drop cap, a small-caps lead-in, a diagram, and margin notes that drop below their paragraph on phones.

**Structure.** A 12-column grid throughout. Pieces are numbered 01–08 across contents, essay and teasers, like a printed contents page. Each teaser has one drawn plate of a shade device.

**Colour.** Limestone paper, ink, terracotta sun, indigo shade. Text passes WCAG AA (mostly AAA) in light and dark modes.

**Access and weight.** Landmarks, skip link, one h1, visible focus, inline form errors, reduced-motion support. No images; fonts about 186 KB, everything else about 23 KB gzipped.
