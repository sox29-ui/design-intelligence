# Brief D — Editorial / cultural publication

**Publication:** *The Lighthouse Review* — a fictional independent quarterly about architecture, cities and the culture of public space, published in print and online. Issue 14 theme: **"Shade"** (how cities in hot climates design for shade).

**Page to design:** the online **issue home page** that also contains the opening of the cover essay.

**Audience:** architects, students, urbanists and curious general readers; roughly half read on phones, often for long stretches. Reading comfort is a priority; the publication should feel authored, not like a news portal or a blog template.

**Content (write plausible copy; all people and places fictional unless generic):**
- Masthead and issue identity (Issue 14 — Shade — Autumn 2026).
- Cover essay: title "The Architecture of Shade", standfirst, author byline (*Leila Haddad*), reading time, and the **first ~700 words of the essay in full** on the page (with at least one pull quote and one subheading).
- Six further articles as teasers across sections (Essays, Projects, Interviews, Archive, Field notes): title, short dek, author, reading time.
- An interview teaser with a fictional landscape architect.
- "From the archive": three past issues.
- Newsletter sign-up (email field) and subscription offer for the print edition.
- Footer with about, contributors, contact, colophon (typefaces used).

**Constraints:**
- Strong typographic hierarchy; comfortable measure and leading for long reading on mobile and desktop.
- Images are optional: use abstract/illustrative placeholders or typographic treatments only (no external photos).
- Accessible structure (headings, landmarks, skip link or equivalent), visible focus, sufficient contrast.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; keep the page light.
