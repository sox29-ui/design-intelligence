# Brief A — Experimental creative landing page

**Project:** *Tidewrack* — a landing page for a fictional sound-art installation that turns live tidal data from the Tagus estuary into a slowly evolving composition. It runs for six weeks (14 Nov 2026 – 27 Dec 2026) in a converted warehouse at a coastal gallery in Lisbon, Portugal.

**Goal:** create intrigue about the work and get visitors to reserve a timed-entry ticket (free, limited capacity).

**Audience:** culturally curious adults, 25–55, most arriving from social media on phones; a minority from press links on desktop.

**Brand personality:** contemplative, slightly uncanny, precise; "the sea as a score". Not a tech product, not a festival.

**Content (use this; you may edit for length, do not invent sponsors or real people):**
- Title: Tidewrack. Line: "A composition written by the tide, performed by the room."
- Three short paragraphs: (1) the idea — tide-gauge readings every 6 minutes are mapped to pitch, density and silence; (2) the space — 40 suspended speakers, visitors walk through the sound; (3) the artist — a fictional composer, *Inês Varela*, born in Setúbal, works with environmental data.
- Practical info: dates, opening hours (Tue–Sun 11:00–19:00), address (Armazém 7, Cais do Sodré, Lisbon), accessibility note (step-free access; seating available; the room is dim).
- Ticket CTA: "Reserve a free timed entry".
- "Low-tide sessions": four special evening performances (28 Nov, 6 Dec, 13 Dec, 20 Dec, 20:30) — each lists the predicted low-tide time (e.g. 20:42, 19:58, 20:16, 21:03).
- Two short press quotes (fictional publications: *Arquivo Sonoro*, *The Listening Post*).
- Partners as text only: Fundação Maré, Lisbon Port Authority (fictional programme), Instituto Hidrográfico (data partner).
- Footer with contact email and social links (placeholders).

**Constraints:**
- Must work well on phones (primary) and desktop.
- Motion is welcome if it serves the concept, but must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Must be usable with keyboard and screen reader; the ticket CTA must be easy to find.
- Keep it light: aim for a fast first load on mobile networks.
