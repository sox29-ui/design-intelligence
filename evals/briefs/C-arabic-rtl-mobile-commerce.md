# Brief C — Arabic RTL mobile-first e-commerce

**Store:** *دكّان القهوة — Dukkan Al-Qahwa*, a fictional specialty-coffee roaster in Riyadh selling beans, drip bags, brewing tools and gift boxes online across Saudi Arabia.

**Page to design:** the store **home page**, Arabic (RTL) first. Arabic is the only required language; an English toggle is optional (if present it must actually switch the page direction and language).

**Audience:** Saudi coffee enthusiasts 20–45, shopping on phones (primary viewport 390 px), often from social links; many compare prices and delivery times before buying.

**Goals:** browse and add to cart quickly; trust the store (delivery, payment, returns); discover the brew guide.

**Content (Arabic; you may write the copy):**
- Header: logo (text), search, cart with item count, menu.
- A seasonal promotion (e.g. new Ethiopian harvest) with a clear call to action.
- Categories: حبوب القهوة، أكياس التقطير، أدوات التحضير، صناديق الهدايا.
- At least 8 product cards with Arabic names that include some Latin terms where natural (e.g. "إثيوبيا غوجي – Natural", "V60"), weight, price in SAR (choose and apply one numeral system consistently and justify it in your notes), rating, and an add-to-cart control. Include one discounted product and one out-of-stock product.
- Free-shipping threshold message (e.g. free delivery over 199 SAR) and delivery-time promise for Riyadh vs other cities.
- A short brew-guide teaser (3 methods).
- Trust section: payment methods (mada, Apple Pay, Tabby — as text labels), returns policy summary, customer service hours.
- Footer: links, social placeholders, commercial registration number placeholder.

**Constraints:**
- Correct RTL behaviour throughout (layout, icons, carousels, numerals, mixed Arabic/Latin text).
- Product browsing must be fast and thumb-friendly; no horizontal page overflow.
- Accessible: labels, focus, contrast, `lang`/`dir` set correctly.
- Light page weight; product images may be simple illustrative placeholders (no external photos).
